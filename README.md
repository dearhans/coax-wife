## 中文

### 这是什么？

一个基于心理学的高情商沟通技能，专门解决中国夫妻 / 情侣的日常冲突、冷战破冰、情绪疏导问题。

**不是鸡汤，不是套路，是心理学框架 + 可执行话术。**

### 理论基础

| 框架 | 来源 | 用在技能里干什么 |
|------|------|----------------|
| **EFT 情绪聚焦疗法** | Sue Johnson | 解码她表面愤怒背后的深层情绪（委屈、不安、被忽视） |
| **Gottman 修复尝试** | John & Julie Gottman | 吵架后怎么开口打破僵局，修复成功率 86% 的策略 |
| **NVC 非暴力沟通** | Marshall Rosenberg | 怎么说话不踩雷，把"你总是"改成"我感到" |
| **追逃模式破解** | EFT + 中国语境 | 一个追着要回应，一个躲着怕吵架，这个死循环怎么破 |

### 功能清单

- **情绪解码**：输入场景 → 分析她真实在想什么
- **三重方案**：温和型 / 幽默型 / 行动型，各配一句「」话术 + 配套行动
- **死亡话术清单**：8 句绝对不能说的话，及替换方案
- **场景速查**：冷战 / 日常忽视 / 工作压力 / 经期 / 育儿分歧 / 婆媳
- **软肋管理**：记住她念叨过的小事，用细节证明心里有她

## English

### What is this?

A psychology-grounded, high-EQ communication skill for navigating couple conflicts. When your partner is upset, it decodes hidden emotions, generates context-aware scripts, and warns you which words will make things worse.

**Not fortune-cookie advice. Structured psychology with executable scripts.**

### Frameworks

| Framework | Origin | How It's Applied |
|-----------|--------|-----------------|
| **EFT** | Sue Johnson | Decode the vulnerable emotions (hurt, fear, loneliness) beneath the surface anger |
| **Gottman Repair** | John & Julie Gottman | 86% success-rate strategies to break the silence after a fight |
| **NVC** | Marshall Rosenberg | Replace "You always..." accusations with "I feel..." vulnerability |
| **Pursuer-Withdrawer** | EFT + cross-cultural | Break the most common couple conflict cycle |

### Features

- **Emotion Decoder**: Describe the situation → understand what she's really feeling
- **3-Path Response**: Gentle / Humorous / Action-oriented scripts with **exact words** and companion actions
- **Death Phrase List**: 8 things you must NEVER say, with safe alternatives
- **Quick Reference**: Cold war / Daily neglect / Work stress / Period days / Parenting / In-law tension
- **Soft Spot Tracker**: Remember the small things she mentioned — and act on them
